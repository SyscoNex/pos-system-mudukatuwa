# Business workflows and posting rules
The following summarizes the core workflows. See the historical technical specification for detailed financial/offline guidance and the new transfer specification for mandatory stock movements.

## Common transaction
Authenticate and resolve context, validate DTO; calculate prices/taxes on server; acquire scoped idempotency record and compare canonical request hash. Lock accounting period first, then original/current documents in stable ID order, then applicable device/register/party credit, then balances/reservations, then cost layers/settlement sources. Document number and new bucket creation must use consistent lock order too; refine with concurrency tests. Use a single connection/transaction for all core changes. Recheck current status, scope, quantity and version under locks. Write typed document root/lines, stock and journal effects, audit/outbox, mark posted, save response and commit. Rollback all on failure. Bounded whole-command retry for recognized deadlock/lock timeout only. A changed payload under existing key conflicts; preserve durable business reference beyond HTTP retry TTL. No external API/printer waits inside transaction.

## Procurement
Requisition (optional) → approved PO → partial goods receipt → QC → supplier invoice matching → payment/settlement. Receive actual quantities/UOM/lots/expiry into warehouse quarantine or approved stock. Create receipt quantities once, with accrued liability/clearing as accounting policy requires; supplier invoice matches receipts and charges, does not receive goods again. Return via separate stock issue and supplier credit with independent financial posting. Match invoices and cumulative receipt/credit quantities; landed cost allocations reconcile and late adjustments follow approved financial policy.

## Manufacturing and quality
Recipe has approved effective version; inputs include raw materials, non-coconut ingredients, bulk product and packaging; outputs may include byproducts. Validate positive conversions, output allocations and no recipe cycles. Release order freezes recipe/plan, warehouse, output target and reserves eligible ingredients. Post actual ingredient issues and returns, output, waste/rework and labor/overhead events. Record actual lot links; record WIP costs and allocate to output under approved policy. Bulk oil can be produced then consumed by separate packaging order with bottles, caps and labels; resulting packs are distinct SKUs and lots. QC output starts quarantined until authorized release. Release/reject/recall atomically updates status and condition; preserve evidence. Recall trace traverses actual input/output and sales/delivery records; identify affected remaining stock and customers without leaking tenant data. Rework/expiry disposal via approved documents. Production never creates sales revenue.

## POS and sales
Open assigned register and cash session; capture items via search/scan, UOM/weight (tare/net evidence), qty, discount, tender and customer if credit. Server validates active SKU, price version, tax rules, branch, permissions, reservation and expiry; requote if changed, no silent price adjustment after capture. Online sale creates invoice with stock issue, FIFO COGS, revenue/tax and AR; confirmed payment is distinct document/tenders allocated to AR. For cash, invoice/payment may commit together. Wholesale order reserves, delivery issues stock, invoice matches delivery without duplicate issue; partial deliveries/invoices. Tax rules, rounding and invoice format approved locally before go-live. Print only after commit; reprint original reference and audit. Return/credit validates original line and remaining return cap under lock; inspection determines restock condition; refund is separately confirmed payment. End shift compares expected cash (opening float + receipts - refunds +/- approved cash movements) to actual counted amount; variance approved and posted as required.

## Payment recovery
Never store PAN/CVV; use provider references and tokens. Payment intent has stable provider idempotency identity. Do not hold transaction awaiting provider. Validate amount/currency and signed callbacks, persist inbox, reconcile provider capture before posting once. Captured-but-unposted payment remains durable recovery state and may need fulfillment/refund; do not silently charge again. Cash and bank transfer/manual card confirmation have controlled permissions and audit; never mark external payment confirmed based solely on browser response.

## Finance
Configure chart and account mappings, functional currency, periods and opening balances. Double-entry journals balanced in both required currency bases; inventory, WIP, AR/AP and tax subledgers reconcile. Example sale accounting (policy dependent): debit AR, credit revenue/output tax; debit COGS, credit inventory; debit cash/clearing, credit AR when payment confirmed. Transfer preserves value without revenue/COGS; production moves raw to WIP/output. Credit/payment/settlement separate; cumulative amounts bounded by source. Use period close lock and approved corrections rather than edits. Maintain accounting reports, outstanding receivables/payables, cash and expenses. Validate local rules with qualified accountant; no asserted tax rates.

## Offline billing (gated pilot feature)
Register managed till/device and secure identity; provision scoped versioned catalog/prices/taxes, permitted offline payment methods, bounded expiry of policy and centrally reserved stock quota per lot/warehouse/device. Local encrypted SQLite transaction records sale, stock quota consumption, receipt identity, monotonic operation sequence/UUID/hash and outbox atomically before receipt. Never issue beyond local quota; online stock sees quota reserved. Use a documented provisional receipt numbering and mapping policy. External card authorization and refunds are online unless provider-approved offline protocol exists. Sync to durable inbox with scoped device authentication, sequence and hash; exact duplicate returns original result, altered replay rejected/audited, gaps wait. Server runs normal posting with quota lock, commits and acknowledges; client marks synced only after ack. Conflicts preserve original cash/stock evidence, show supervisor resolution state, and require approved compensating action. Do not auto-release disconnected quotas; reconcile acknowledged operations and unused quota, including lost/revoked devices. Feed uses serialized committed sequence/checkpoint; snapshot plus replay, versioned tombstones. Offline restrictions and expiry/recall delay disclosed to client, and tested before enablement.
