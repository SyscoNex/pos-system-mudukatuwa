## Approved posting policies

- Enter the actual cost per selected unit explicitly. Base unit cost is the entered cost divided by the exact factor, rounded to six decimals, midpoint away from zero, for reference. The carried line value is entered quantity times entered unit cost, rounded once to six decimals, midpoint away from zero. Never recompute value from the rounded base cost. Sum carried line values for the document. Round that sum to currency minor units for display only. The bucket adds exact base quantity and carried value. Its average is derived as value divided by quantity. Future proportional issues take exact remaining value on final depletion.
- Zero entered cost requires explicit line confirmation and is identifiable in posted evidence and audit. First posted cost locks company currency, including when the cost is zero.
- Use the configured local date. An expiry date is inclusive. Expired stock may enter HOLD or DAMAGED only, even on a backdated receipt. A recorded business date may not be future-dated. Manufacture may not follow business date or expiry. A reused lot must match existing dates and supplier lot reference. A non-tracked item uses the canonical SYSTEM lot and has no batch dates. Lot codes are case-sensitive and scoped by item.
- Opening stock is Administrator-only and permitted once per product and warehouse, with all initial lots entered together. Any prior movement for that product and warehouse blocks further opening. Supplier reference is optional evidence and is not the replay key.
- Staff with an active outlet grant may enter actual receiving costs and confirm their own input in preview. Posting responses and subsequent inventory, document and history reads omit historical cost and value fields for Staff. Administrators may read those costs. Staff without grants cannot use inventory.
- Numbering uses OPEN or GRN with the business date year and a transaction-owned counter. Posting uses current session and actor/company locks, verifies warehouse scope and versions, resolves replay, verifies current catalogue and product/warehouse opening eligibility, allocates the sequence, then writes lots, buckets, document/lines, entries and projections, audit and durable result. Company serialization follows the existing organization and catalogue guard. Warehouse is explicit and must match the selected outlet if one is set. Consistent key order is used when inspecting balances. Any failure rolls the entire transaction back.
- Reconciliation compares ledger quantity and value to each balance under one consistent transaction. It reports discrepancies for review. Rebuilding a projection is a controlled recovery procedure.
